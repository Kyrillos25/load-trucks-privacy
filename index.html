Load Trucks privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026. Developer: geekbug. Contact: kyrillos25@gmail.com.

Your age

At the first start the game asks for your year of birth. The year itself is not saved. The game only keeps an age group (under 13, 13 to 15, or older) on your device and does not send it to us. It decides which features you get, as described below.

Children under 13

For players who say they are under 13:

  • there is no online play: no account, no name, no leaderboard, and no progress is sent to us or to Unity's online services;
  • there is no shop for real money;
  • ads are requested with Google's treatment for children, so they are not personalized, and only with the content rating G.

Their progress is stored only on the device (and in the device's backup, if it is on). Google may still process technical data, such as the IP address and device information, to show and measure these ads and to prevent abuse. Parents can write to kyrillos25@gmail.com with questions or to have data deleted.

What stays on your device

Your progress, coins, power-ups, lives, purchase references and settings are stored on your device. As long as you do not play online, we do not receive them. If backup is on, Android may store a copy in your Google account, or an iPhone in your iCloud account, so your progress returns on a new device. You can turn this off in the settings of your device.

Online play (13 and older)

Online play starts only if you choose a name and tap "Play online". Then an account without a password is created, and an account number, your name, your progress (including coins, power-ups and purchase references) and your scores are stored with Unity Gaming Services. Other players see your name and your scores on the leaderboards. Use a name that does not reveal who you are. See the Unity privacy policy.

"Delete my online data" in your profile deletes your saved progress and your account. Your scores may stay on the leaderboards, but under the name "Deleted" instead of yours. You can also ask us to delete your data.

Purchases (13 and older)

Coins, power-ups, the monthly pass and the removal of ads can be bought through Google Play or the App Store. Google or Apple processes the payment; we never see your card or bank details. The game uses Unity In-App Purchasing, which may process data such as purchase details, device information and identifiers. See the Google privacy policy, the Apple privacy policy and the Unity privacy policy.

Ads

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. For players of 16 and older, Google may collect and use data such as device identifiers, the advertising ID (on Android), the approximate location (from the IP address) and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads, and to personalize them where this is allowed. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice under "Ad choices" in the settings. For players of 13 to 15, ads are requested with Google's treatment for teenagers. For children, see above. "Remove ads" stops the ads between levels; ads you choose to watch for a reward stay available. On iPhones the game does not ask for permission to track you, does not use the advertising identifier and only requests ads that are not personalized. See how Google uses this data.

What we do not do

The game does not ask for access to your precise location, contacts, camera or microphone. We do not sell your data.

Deleting your data

Online data: use "Delete my online data" in your profile, or ask us. On your phone: uninstall the game, or clear its data in the Android settings, and delete the backup in your Google or iCloud account. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to see, correct or move your data, or to complain to a data protection authority. Write to kyrillos25@gmail.com.
