Delete your Load Trucks data

Load Trucks is a game by geekbug. You can delete your online account and data at any time.

In the game

  1. Open the game and tap the profile button on the home screen.
  2. Tap "Delete my online data", and again to confirm.

This deletes your account and your saved progress at once. Your scores may stay on the leaderboards under the name "Deleted".

Without the game

Write to kyrillos25@gmail.com and give the name you play with and your highest level, so we can find the right account. Please do not send passwords or copies of documents. We delete your account, your saved progress and your leaderboard entries within 30 days.

What is deleted, and what is not

Deleted: the account, the name and the saved progress. Not with us: records of purchases, which Google Play keeps, and what Google processed to show ads. See the privacy policy.
