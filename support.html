Load Trucks support

Load Trucks is a game by geekbug. Questions, problems and ideas are welcome: write to the contact address at the top of the privacy policy. Please say which phone you use and which level you were playing.

My purchase did not arrive

Open the game with an internet connection and wait a moment; paid purchases are delivered at the next start. On an iPhone you can also tap "Restore purchases" in the settings to bring back "Remove ads". If it still does not arrive, write to us. Please do not send card details or passwords.

I want my money back

Payments are handled by Google Play or the App Store. Ask for a refund there, or write to us if you need help.

My progress on a new phone

If your phone's backup is on, progress usually comes back with it. Online progress belongs to the phone it was made on. Progress cannot be moved between Android and iPhone yet.

A level cannot be won

Every level has a solution. Power-ups and "undo" help when you are stuck.

Children

Parents can read in the privacy policy what is different for players under 13.
